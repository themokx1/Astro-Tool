Privacy

Short, because there isn't much to say: AstroTool works on your machine, and stays there.

Guide · CLI · Features

Everything local

Scanning your library, the index, the measurements and the analyses all run on your own machine. No account, no registration, no telemetry, no ads. The app is fully functional without an internet connection.

What leaves your machine, if you allow it

Two features request data over the network, and both can be turned off in Settings:

  • Weather: off by default. If you turn it on, the observing site's coordinates go to Open-Meteo for the cloud-cover forecast. No filename, no target, no other data.
  • Extended target catalog: on by default, and it fetches missing data on its own the first time you open Discovery if there's no local catalog yet. When it does, catalog names and sky coordinates go to SIMBAD/VizieR, nothing else. The downloaded catalog is stored locally and works offline afterward; you can turn this off in Settings at any time.

The contents of your library, its paths, files, and notes never leave your machine.

Diagnostics

A diagnostics report is only created when you ask for it in Settings, and you decide who you give it to. It contains the version number, system info, anonymous counts, and operation categories. No paths, filenames, targets, coordinates, or notes are in it.

Your images stay safe

The app only reads your image library by default. Every write operation sits behind its own toggle and asks for confirmation before each write. Instead of deleting, it quarantines: whatever disappears from its spot can be brought back.
