AstroTool 4.0 · prepare at home

Plan the complete night on your Mac. Carry only the plan into the field.

Night Briefing is a calm five-step workflow. It produces the same detailed plan as a selectable-text A4 PDF and, if requested, 144 DPI PNG pages that are comfortable to read on a phone.

It does not guess or touch your photos.Missing or stale data stays visibly marked. Export creates new files, never overwrites an existing destination, and does not delete or move anything.
Before you begin

Have these three decisions nearby.

01

The night's limits

Date, site, expected arrival and departure. Leave anything you do not yet know blank.

02

Your real equipment

The setup, filter, and power you intend to use. Calibration settings must match this plan.

03

A primary decision

At least one target. Add a backup only if it is genuinely workable.

The five steps

You always know where you are and what is missing.

  1. 1
    Basics

    Set the date, site, arrival, departure, setup, and expected power runtime. Check the weather retrieval time too; a forecast is never a guarantee.

  2. 2
    Targets

    Place the primary and optional backup target into time windows. Starting from Planning carries over the selected date, setup, site, and verified sky path.

  3. 3
    Capture plan

    For each target, enter filter, exposure, frame count, gain, offset, binning, and temperature. AstroTool does not guess a value for a blank field.

  4. 4
    Checklist and backup plan

    Hide items that do not apply, add personal items, then review actions for delay, a shorter night, cloud, calibration gaps, and declining image quality.

  5. 5
    Review and export

    Ready, Worth checking, and Still incomplete show what is known and what needs attention. Save a PDF, PDF + PNG pages, or PNG pages only.

Inside the document

Ordered for use in the field.

Cover and readiness, night timeline, target sheets, sky path and darkness, equipment and calibration, checklist, backup plan, and a blank field-notes page.

What do the warnings mean?

Ready: the required decisions are present.

Worth checking: export is safe, but there may be overlap, stale weather, or a calibration gap.

Still incomplete: important context is missing. Only a missing date or invalid time window blocks export.

Field use

It stays with you offline.

A4 PDF

Selectable text and printable pages with no external font, image, script, or network dependency.

144 DPI PNG

Every PDF page becomes a separate image, so the phone copy always matches the canonical document.

Immutable revisions

Saving creates a new revision. An earlier version remains untouched and can be continued later.

Before leaving

The PDF is a plan, not automatic control.

Refresh the weather, inspect the real sky, verify equipment safety, and match calibration. If reality differs, stop and decide from the conditions in front of you.
